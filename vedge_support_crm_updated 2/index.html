    How your support tools work together

    Shopify · Customer & order record

    1. Search by order number or customer email.
    2. Confirm customer, line items, discount, payment/refund history and fulfillment status.
    3. Review order timeline and shipping address.
    4. Before editing/canceling/refunding, confirm the order is still eligible and check ShipMonk status.
    5. Re-open the order after the action to verify the result.

    ShipMonk · Fulfillment & tracking

    1. Search using the Shopify order/reference number supported by your account.
    2. Review fulfillment state, package/tracking IDs, carrier scans and exceptions.
    3. For delivered-not-received, inspect proof of delivery if available.
    4. For address changes/cancellations, determine whether the warehouse has already processed/shipped the order.
    5. Document each parcel for split or duplicate shipment cases.

    Loop Subscriptions · Recurring orders

    1. Find the subscriber using the confirmed account email.
    2. Inspect active subscriptions, products, quantities, cadence, next charge and status.
    3. Check for duplicate subscriptions before changing anything.
    4. Make only the customer-authorized change: pause, skip, cancel, swap, quantity or cadence.
    5. Verify the saved subscription and next shipment/charge details.

    Quality checks, guardrails & escalation

    Before you reply

    • Confirm the correct customer and order.
    • Check current status in the right system.
    • Check whether the requested action is reversible/allowed.
    • Get customer confirmation for ambiguous or material changes.
    • Verify the saved change/refund/tracking information.
    • State what happened and what happens next.

    Escalate instead of guessing

    • Product defect, unusual color/odor, contamination concern or adverse reaction.
    • Allergy, medication, pregnancy or medical-condition questions.
    • Missing/damaged parcel with no clear carrier resolution.
    • Refund/discount outside your authorization.
    • Wholesale, affiliate or partnership commitments.
    • System mismatch between Shopify, ShipMonk and Loop.
    Policy-sensitive details in the uploaded history: 3–5 business-day refund posting; 30% EARLY non-stacking; 15% Subscribe & Save; domestic $99 free-shipping threshold; 30/45/60-day subscription cadence; product facts and discontinued flavors; returns address. These are historical examples from the file—not guaranteed current policy. Verify before quoting.
