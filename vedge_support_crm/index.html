    How to use your support platforms

    Front · Email communication

    1. Open the customer conversation in the support inbox.
    2. Read the full thread, not only the latest message or export excerpt.
    3. Identify the exact request and collect the order number/account email if missing.
    4. Use internal notes for investigation; keep internal details out of the customer reply.
    5. Send the response only after the relevant checks and actions are complete.

    Shopify · Orders & customers

    1. Search using the order number or customer email from Front.
    2. Verify customer, line items, discounts, payment, refunds, address and fulfillment status.
    3. Review order timeline and confirm whether the order is editable or already fulfilled.
    4. Before canceling, editing or refunding, check the related ShipMonk and/or Loop status.
    5. Re-open the order after an authorized action to verify the result.

    ShipMonk · Tracking & fulfillment

    1. Search the shipment using the order/reference supported by your account.
    2. Review fulfillment status, tracking number, carrier scans and exception notes.
    3. For delivered-not-received, check proof of delivery if available and follow the missing-package SOP.
    4. For address changes or cancellations, check whether warehouse processing or carrier handoff has occurred.
    5. Document shipment findings and escalate exceptions according to team procedure.

    Loop Subscriptions · Recurring orders

    1. Find the subscriber using the confirmed account email.
    2. Review active subscriptions, products, quantities, cadence, next charge and status.
    3. Check for duplicate subscriptions before changing anything.
    4. Make only the customer-authorized change: pause, skip, cancel, reactivate, swap, quantity or cadence.
    5. Verify the saved subscription and check Shopify/ShipMonk if an order is already placed.
